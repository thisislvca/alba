Different phones.
Same shared albums.

When your family has iPhones and you have an Android, you can still see the photos they add to your shared albums. Open the albums you already use, with the Apple Account you already have.

About shared albums ↗

Photos, videos and Live Photos.

Browse your library, play a Live Photo and find your favourites. All in a native Android app.

Keep your existing storage.

Use the cloud subscription you already have. Your library stays where it is, without moving everything to another service.

Save originals offline.

Download photos and videos to your phone, then open or share them when you’re offline.

Your phone connects to Apple.

Alba has no server that handles your photos or sign-in details. Your session is stored encrypted on your phone.

Free and open source.

Alba has no paid tier or app subscription. The code is on GitHub, where you can report a bug, suggest a feature or contribute.
