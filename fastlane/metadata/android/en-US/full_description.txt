Alba brings your iCloud photo library to Android. Browse your photos and videos, back up your phone to iCloud and keep using your Shared Albums, without a Mac or an iPhone at hand.

YOUR LIBRARY
• Browse your iCloud Photos library alongside the photos on your phone
• Watch videos and Live Photos
• Find photos by file name, caption, album, month or type
• Use albums, Favorites, Hidden and Recently Deleted

BACKUP
• Back up photos and videos from your camera and the folders you choose
• Include the photos and videos already on your phone, newest first
• Originals go straight to iCloud and are checked there before they count as backed up
• Uses Wi-Fi unless you allow mobile data; interrupted uploads retry on their own

SHARED ALBUMS
• Create shared albums, invite people and add photos and videos
• Join albums shared with you, comment and react

ON YOUR PHONE
• Keep originals offline, save them to your gallery or share them with other apps
• Crop, rotate, adjust and filter photos, then save an edited copy

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your phone connects directly to Apple. Alba has no server for your photos or credentials, never saves your password and has no ads or behavioral analytics. Your sign-in is kept encrypted on your phone.

TRY IT FIRST
The demo works without an account, with sample photos and a simulated backup.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Requires Android 11 or later and an Apple Account with iCloud Photos turned on
• Supports standard two-factor authentication. Accounts that use security keys or Advanced Data Protection aren’t supported yet, and neither is iCloud Shared Photo Library
• Alba is experimental. It uses Apple’s unofficial web APIs, so features can break when Apple changes them

Alba is free and open source (AGPL-3.0), with no subscriptions or paid features. It is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Apple Inc. iCloud is a trademark of Apple Inc.
